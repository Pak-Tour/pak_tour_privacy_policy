# PakTour Privacy Policy

**Last updated: 10 November 2026**

PakTour (also displayed as “Pak Tour”) is provided by Nexus4Tech (“we”, “us”, or “our”). This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, store and share information when you use our Android and iOS applications and contact us about the service.

PakTour helps passengers and drivers find intercity journeys, offer or request seats, communicate and manage trips. Our application identifier is **com.nexus.paktour**.

**Privacy contact:** [nexus4techofficial@gmail.com](mailto:nexus4techofficial@gmail.com)

## 1. Information we collect

### Account and profile information

When you sign in with Google, we receive account information such as your Google account identifier, email address, name and profile picture. We do not receive your Google password.

We store a PakTour account identifier and the profile information you provide, including your display name, home city, mobile number and profile photo. A mobile number is required to complete the current profile setup; it is a contact number rather than a phone sign-in credential.

### Journey, booking and communication information

We collect information you submit through the service, including:

- Departure and destination cities, travel dates, specific or flexible time preferences, and selected pickup or departure points, including their names and coordinates.
- Seats offered or requested, vehicle descriptions, fares or budgets, and journey notes.
- Ride alerts and saved matching preferences.
- Seat requests, offers, booking decisions, cancellations, seat changes, and trip start or completion records.
- Chat messages, conversation participants and message timestamps.
- Ratings, reports, report reasons and information you send to support.

Information entered in a free-text field may contain personal information. Only include information needed to arrange your journey.

### Location and map information

The current app lets you select cities and places manually or by tapping a map. We store the locations you save with a post or search and use them to show pickup points, nearby journeys and distances. A selected point can reveal a precise location even though it was entered manually.

The current app does not request device GPS location permission or continuously track your background location. A map pin or distance is not live tracking of a person or vehicle.

Map requests can reveal the area being viewed to the map provider. Where online place search or reverse lookup is used, the query or selected coordinates are sent to the relevant place-search provider. Providers also receive technical information such as an IP address when your device connects to their services.

### Device, notification and technical information

Our app and service providers process technical information such as app version, device model, operating system, language, IP address, app or installation identifiers, notification registration tokens, service logs and error information.

We use Firebase Analytics to understand app interactions and Firebase Crashlytics to diagnose crashes and errors. These services can receive usage events, diagnostic information and identifiers. We associate the PakTour account identifier with analytics and crash reporting for troubleshooting and understanding service usage.

### Advertising information

PakTour displays ads using Google AdMob / the Google Mobile Ads SDK. The SDK can collect and share IP addresses, approximate location inferred from IP addresses, app and ad interactions, diagnostics, and device or account identifiers, including the Android advertising ID where available and permitted by the operating system.

Our current ad requests are marked as non-personalized. Non-personalized ads still involve data processing for purposes such as delivering ads, measurement, frequency control, security and fraud prevention; they do not mean that no data is collected.

### Information stored on your device

We store settings and caches on your device, such as language, theme, time format, passenger/driver mode, recent routes, selected search locations, onboarding state and cached profile or listing information. Authentication services also maintain information needed to keep you signed in.

## 2. How we use information

We use information to:

- Create accounts, authenticate users and maintain profiles.
- Publish and find ride offers and passenger travel posts, display maps and calculate distances.
- Enable chat, seat requests, booking management, ratings and reports.
- Send messages, booking updates, matching alerts and departure reminders, subject to notification permissions and delivery availability.
- Operate, maintain, secure and troubleshoot the app, and understand how features are used.
- Deliver and measure advertising and detect invalid traffic.
- Respond to support and privacy requests, investigate misuse and resolve disputes.
- Meet applicable legal obligations and protect users and the service.

We do not process ride fares or collect payment-card details in the current version. Any fare payment is arranged directly between the people travelling.

## 3. When information is shared

### With other PakTour users

Ride offers and passenger travel posts are intended to be seen by other signed-in users. They can include your name, profile image, rating, route, travel date/time, pickup or departure point, vehicle details, seat counts, price and notes.

Profile records are currently accessible to signed-in users and can include your email address, mobile number, name, profile image and city. Your email is not displayed as a listing field, but you should not treat it as inaccessible to other account holders. Your mobile number may be used by other users to contact you about a journey.

Chat messages and booking details are available to the people participating in that conversation or booking. Authorized administrators may access relevant records for support, safety, dispute handling and service operation. Chats are not end-to-end encrypted.

Other users may save or copy information you share. Removing content or deleting an account cannot remove copies already retained by another person. Choose public pickup points rather than a home address, and avoid sharing identification documents, passwords or financial details in posts or chats.

### With service providers

We use the following categories of providers:

- **Google Sign-In and Firebase Authentication:** account access and authentication.
- **Google Firebase / Google Cloud:** database, image storage, backend processing, notifications, configuration, usage analytics and crash reporting.
- **Google AdMob and advertising partners involved in serving its ads:** ad delivery, measurement, security and fraud prevention.
- **OpenFreeMap:** map styles and map tiles used to display geographic areas.
- **OpenStreetMap Nominatim, where online place lookup is used:** processing place-search queries or selected coordinates to return place names.
- **Apple and Google platform services:** notification delivery, operating-system functionality and store distribution, as applicable.

These providers process information needed for their services under applicable agreements and their own privacy terms. Relevant information is available at:

- [Google Privacy Policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy)
- [Firebase Privacy and Security](https://firebase.google.com/support/privacy)
- [Google Advertising Information](https://policies.google.com/technologies/ads)
- [Google Mobile Ads SDK Data Disclosures](https://developers.google.com/admob/android/privacy/play-data-disclosure)
- [OpenFreeMap](https://openfreemap.org/)
- [OpenStreetMap Foundation Privacy Policy](https://osmfoundation.org/wiki/Privacy_Policy)
- [Apple Privacy Policy](https://www.apple.com/legal/privacy/)

We do not sell your personal information. We do not provide your chat text or selected pickup coordinates to advertisers as an advertising audience profile.

### For legal, safety and business reasons

We may disclose information where reasonably necessary to comply with applicable law or a valid legal request, investigate fraud or abuse, enforce our terms, or protect rights and safety. If ownership or operation of PakTour changes, relevant information may be transferred to the successor, subject to applicable safeguards and notice requirements.

## 4. Permissions and your choices

- **Notifications:** You can allow or disable notifications through your device settings. Essential in-app functionality remains available, but you may miss timely messages and alerts when notifications are disabled.
- **Profile photos:** If you choose a photo, the app uses the system photo-selection flow and uploads the selected image. It does not need unrestricted access to your entire photo library to provide this feature.
- **Profile and posts:** You can edit supported profile fields and manage your posts in the app. A confirmed booking may restrict changes until you follow the booking cancellation or completion process.
- **Ride alerts:** You can stop saved searches through the ride/passenger alert feature.
- **Advertising settings:** Your device may let you delete or limit its advertising identifier or control advertising permissions. The available controls depend on the device and operating-system version.
- **External apps:** Opening a phone dialer, email app, navigation app or external website shares information needed for the action you choose with that service. Its own privacy policy then applies.

## 5. Storage, retention and security

We keep account and service information while your account is active and as needed to provide the service. Journey and communication records can remain after a departure date or after a post stops appearing in searches. Expiry or removal from search is not permanent deletion from storage.

Following account deletion, historical conversations, bookings, ratings, reports, inactive listings, uploaded images and some technical records may remain. Retention depends on the type of record and whether it is needed for another participant's history, support, safety, dispute resolution, fraud prevention, legal obligations or the operation of our providers' backup and retention systems. Not all retained records are automatically anonymized. Contact us to request deletion of additional identifiable content.

We do not promise a fixed automatic deletion period for these historical records. We assess additional deletion requests against the continuing need to retain the information and applicable requirements. Our service providers also apply their own retention and backup practices.

We use encrypted network connections and access controls to protect information. Access depends on the feature: posts and profile information have broader signed-in-user access than private conversations. No storage or transmission method can guarantee absolute security.

## 6. Account deletion and privacy requests

To delete your account in the app, open **Home → Menu → Delete account** and confirm the request.

The current deletion process removes your Firebase Authentication account, anonymizes core profile details, clears notification tokens, releases linked phone claims, removes saved ride alerts and takes active listings out of the marketplace. As explained above, it does not automatically erase every historical record or uploaded image. Uninstalling the app alone does not delete your account or cloud data.

You can also request account deletion or deletion of additional personal information without using the app by emailing **[nexus4techofficial@gmail.com](mailto:nexus4techofficial@gmail.com)** with the subject **“PakTour account deletion”**. Include the email address associated with your PakTour account and describe your request. Do not send your Google password. We may ask for reasonable verification before acting to protect accounts from unauthorized deletion.

You may contact the same address to request access to or correction of your information, ask about retention, or exercise privacy rights available under applicable law. We will explain where a request cannot be fully completed because information must be retained or belongs to another participant's records.

## 7. International processing

PakTour is currently focused on Pakistan. Our Firebase database and backend are configured in Google's Mumbai region, but Google and other service providers may process or store some information in other countries. Processing locations and safeguards depend on the services involved and applicable provider agreements.

PakTour is intended for adults aged 18 and over and is not directed at children. We do not knowingly seek to collect children's personal information. If you believe a child has provided information through PakTour, contact us so we can investigate and take appropriate action.
