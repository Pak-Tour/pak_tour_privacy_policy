## Who we are

Pak Tour (“we”) provides an intercity ride-sharing marketplace app for travelers in Pakistan.

## Data we collect

- Google account identifiers (email, name, photo) when you sign in
- Profile details you provide (display name, city, optional phone, photo)
- Listings, chat messages, bookings, ratings, and reports you create
- Device push tokens for notifications
- App language and theme preferences stored on device
- Basic diagnostics if Crashlytics / Analytics are enabled in a release build

## How we use data

- Provide marketplace, chat, booking, rating, and safety features
- Send transactional push notifications (seat requests, messages, etc.)
- Enforce trust & safety (reports, bans)
- Improve reliability and prevent abuse

## Sharing

We use Google Firebase (Auth, Firestore, Storage, Cloud Messaging, Cloud Functions). We do not sell personal data. We may disclose information when required by law or to protect users’ safety.

## Retention & deletion

You can delete your account in the app (Me → Delete account). We anonymize your profile, remove your Auth login, and take down active listings. Messages visible to other parties may remain for their records.
