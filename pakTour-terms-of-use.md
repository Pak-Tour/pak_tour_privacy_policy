## The service

PakTour is a marketplace that helps travelers find seats or fill empty seats on intercity trips. We are **not** a taxi company, carrier, or insurer. Agreements are between travelers.

## Your responsibilities

- Provide accurate profile and listing information

- Comply with local laws while traveling
- Do not spam, scam, harass, or post fake listings

## Trust & safety

We may remove content, restrict accounts, or ban users who violate these terms or community safety expectations. You can report problems from listings and chats.
